# Mission planet photographs

Photographic spacecraft/telescope imagery, cropped around the full planetary disk and resized to 320 × 320 WebP. No generated planet illustrations or colour overlays.

Mission imagery may combine camera exposures and use the source institution's published colour processing. These are observational images, not claims of unprocessed naked-eye colour.
